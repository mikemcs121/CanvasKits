# Canvas kits

Current production sets after the September 21, 2026 original-art restoration. Each kit root contains exactly the three linked files. Guide PDFs are one US Letter page; reference PDFs and SVG transfers are 8 by 10 inches. Print artwork at Actual size / 100%. All existing production SVG geometry is preserved at 2-point gray.

Four references (Fall Gnome, Christmas Gnome, Fall View and Sunflower) and their guides were restored; Flowers in Vase and Gnome Halloween received guide-only detail cleanup. All outlines, originals and the other four kits remain unchanged. [Restoration review and comparisons](reference-restoration-review.md) · [Completed handoff](RESTORATION-HANDOFF.md).

All ten guides now follow the Fall View template, with logos and background accents matched to their individual paintings. [Guide update and color details](guide-template-refactor.md).

## Review and repairs

Fox Fall also has a separate simplified edition for ages 8-14. It retains original stages 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 as six child-friendly panels. The native stage-7 paws group is black in the guide and flat-color reference; every pixel outside that mask remains unchanged. Advanced texture, shading and highlight stages are omitted. Six ready-to-use colors are provided, dark brown is not used, and no mixing is required. The production set above is preserved. Simplified files: [unchanged outline copy](<Fox Fall/simplified/fox-fall-outline-8x10.svg>), [six-step guide](<Fox Fall/simplified/fox-fall-painting-guide-8x10.pdf>), and [simplified reference](<Fox Fall/simplified/fox-fall-finished-reference-8x10.pdf>). Supporting sources and checks are linked in the kit's [info README](<Fox Fall/info/README.md>).

Cat in Pumpkin also has a separate simplified edition for ages 8-14, retaining original steps 1, 2, 3 and 6 as four numbered panels. Its reference follows the original step-6 painting with the formerly golden eye areas left white, per the user's September 23 revision. Six ready-to-use paint colors are provided; no mixing is required. The existing production set above is preserved. Simplified files: [unchanged outline copy](<Cat in Pumpkin/simplified/cat-in-pumpkin-outline-8x10.svg>), [four-step guide](<Cat in Pumpkin/simplified/cat-in-pumpkin-painting-guide-8x10.pdf>), and [simplified reference](<Cat in Pumpkin/simplified/cat-in-pumpkin-finished-reference-8x10.pdf>). Supporting sources and checks are linked in the kit's [info README](<Cat in Pumpkin/info/README.md>).

Pumpkin also has a separate simplified edition for ages 8-14 with four child-friendly panels based on original stages 1-4. It uses a flat-color target and only three ready-to-use paints: orange, olive green and black. Mixing, groove shadows, painted texture and highlights are omitted. The approved centered composition and original production set are preserved. Simplified files: [unchanged outline copy](<Pumpkin/simplified/pumpkin-outline-8x10.svg>), [four-step guide](<Pumpkin/simplified/pumpkin-painting-guide-8x10.pdf>), and [simplified reference](<Pumpkin/simplified/pumpkin-finished-reference-8x10.pdf>). Supporting sources and checks are linked in the kit's [info README](<Pumpkin/info/README.md>).

Ghost Fall also has a separate simplified edition for ages 8-14 with five child-friendly panels. It uses a flat-color target, keeps the established centered composition, and omits mixing, fold shading, texture and highlights. Six ready-to-use colors are provided. The production set above is preserved. Simplified files: [unchanged outline copy](<Ghost Fall/simplified/ghost-fall-outline-8x10.svg>), [five-step guide](<Ghost Fall/simplified/ghost-fall-painting-guide-8x10.pdf>), and [simplified reference](<Ghost Fall/simplified/ghost-fall-finished-reference-8x10.pdf>). Supporting sources and checks are linked in the kit's [info README](<Ghost Fall/info/README.md>).

During organization, the Christmas Gnome, Fall Gnome, Fall View and Sunflower references/guides were updated to match their existing portrait outlines, and Pumpkin’s clipped guide was repaired. The subsequent template refactor replaced all ten guide layouts. Fox Fall, Ghost Fall, Cat in Pumpkin and Pumpkin keep their existing centered arrangements. Fox Fall and Cat in Pumpkin use children’s #808080 gray; all other kits use #A6A6A6.
