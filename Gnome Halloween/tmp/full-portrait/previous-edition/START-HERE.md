# Halloween Gnome — 8 x 10 edition

The current transfer file is [gnome-halloween-outline-8x10.svg](output/svg/8x10/gnome-halloween-outline-8x10.svg): true black vector paths with fully transparent background and interiors, sized 8 x 10 inches. Upload the SVG for resolution-independent linework.

The matching [vector outline PDF](output/pdf/8x10/gnome-halloween-outline-8x10.pdf) is also exactly 8 x 10 inches. Print at Actual size / 100%. Transparent PNG exports are available at 300 and 600 DPI.

The [illustrated guide](output/pdf/8x10/gnome-halloween-painting-guide-8x10.pdf) is one US Letter page. Its matching PNG is rendered directly from the PDF. The [finished reference](output/pdf/8x10/gnome-halloween-finished-reference-8x10.png) has a matching 8 x 10 PDF in the same folder.

The supplied test outline is the fixed geometry master. The portrait reference adapts the existing adult plum/russet treatment; its texture is generated and resampled for print. Seven paints: white, black, red, yellow, blue, green and brown. Original square artwork and guide are unchanged.

Editable build sources, masks and verification are in `tmp/vector-8x10/`; illustrations are in `assets/8x10/`.
