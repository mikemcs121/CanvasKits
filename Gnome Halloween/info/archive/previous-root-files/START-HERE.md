# Halloween Gnome — 8 x 10 edition

The current transfer file is [gnome-halloween-outline-8x10.svg](output/svg/8x10/gnome-halloween-outline-8x10.svg): true black vector paths with fully transparent background and interiors, sized 8 x 10 inches. Upload the SVG for resolution-independent linework.

The matching [vector outline PDF](output/pdf/8x10/gnome-halloween-outline-8x10.pdf) is also exactly 8 x 10 inches. Print at Actual size / 100%. Transparent PNG exports are available at 300 and 600 DPI.

The [illustrated guide](output/pdf/8x10/gnome-halloween-painting-guide-8x10.pdf) is one US Letter page. Its matching PNG is rendered directly from the PDF. The [finished reference](output/pdf/8x10/gnome-halloween-finished-reference-8x10.png) has a matching 8 x 10 PDF in the same folder.

The current outline is a recomposed portrait with a taller hat, longer lower silhouette, raised moon and bats, and lowered pumpkins and shoes. Its visible design occupies 95.7% of the canvas height, compared with 82.3% in the rejected edition. This is a subject-layout correction, not extra background around the old arrangement.

The earlier square sources, guide and rejected exports are preserved in `tmp/full-portrait/previous-edition/`; the original test outline remains in `test/` as a source only. Current root outline/reference aliases and the familiar illustrated-guide filenames now open the corrected portrait edition.

The reference keeps the adult plum/russet treatment; its texture is generated and resampled for print. Seven paints: white, black, red, yellow, blue, green and brown.

Current build sources, masks and verification are in `tmp/full-portrait/`; illustrations are in `assets/full-portrait/`. The vector geometry was revised by `tmp/vector-8x10/recompose.cjs`. Earlier `tmp/vector-8x10/` export scripts are superseded. The Desktop SVG in `Canvas Kit Vector Outlines` is refreshed with this edition.
