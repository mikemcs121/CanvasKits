Hokie Sunset

8-Color Test Guide
Materials: Preprinted 10 x 8 horizontal canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes
Water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate

8 ready-to-use paints supplied - match the pot numbers below. No mixing recipes.

1Cream
2Peach
3Orange
4Light blue
5Medium blue
6Dark blue
7Maroon
8White

1. Paint the cream sky

Start with the preprinted canvas. Use 1 Cream around the emblem, above the mountains. Keep the emblem and mountain bands white. Brush thin coats. Rinse and blot between colors.

2. Add the ready sunset

Use 2 Peach across the middle sky and 3 Orange just above the mountains. Feather the touching edges with light sideways strokes. Use the supplied paints directly; no mixing on the palette. Let dry.

3. Paint the far ridge

Use 4 Light blue for the highest mountain band. Follow the printed ridge with the small brush, then fill to the next line. Keep the lower bands and emblem white. Let dry.

4. Add the middle ridge

Fill the middle mountain band with 5 Medium blue. Follow the curves with sideways strokes. Keep the nearest mountain and emblem white. Let dry before painting beside it.

5. Fill the nearest ridge

Use 6 Dark blue in the lowest mountain band, right to the bottom edge. Keep its upper edge clear. One even color is enough; no shadows or highlights are needed. Let dry.

6. Paint the maroon

Use 7 Maroon in the emblem and its small inner marks. Leave the turkey details and narrow border white. Use two thin coats, drying between them. Turn the canvas for control.

7. Frame and finish

Paint the narrow border with 3 Orange, including both sides and the top of the open V/T gap. Let dry. Fill the turkey details with 8 White. Touch up edges using the same numbered paints.

8. Dry and clean up

Lay the finished canvas flat until fully dry. Wash and reshape both brushes, wipe your palette and close the paints. Print the transfer and reference at Actual size / 100%.
