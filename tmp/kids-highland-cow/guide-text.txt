Kids Canvas Painting Guide
You will need: Preprinted 8 x 10 canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes
Water, paper towels, and a palette or paper plate
Ready-to-use paints: light blue, caramel tan, cream, pink,
brown, black, and white. No mixing needed.
1. Blue background
Start with your preprinted
canvas. Use a large brush to
paint light blue around the cow.
Use a small brush near the
edges. Keep the cow white. Use
thin coats. Rinse and blot your
brush, then let the paint dry.
2. Fluffy tan fur
Paint the hair, face, outer ears,
and body caramel tan. Brush in
the direction of the fur. Keep the
horns, muzzle, bow, and eyes
white. Leave a small white curve
inside each ear for pink. Let the
tan dry.
3. Cream horns and muzzle
Use a small brush to paint both
horns cream. Paint the large
muzzle cream too. Leave the two
small nostrils white for now.
Keep the eyes and bow white.
Rinse and blot your brush. Let
the cream dry.
4. A pretty pink bow
Paint the bow pink, including its
knot and hanging ends. Add a
pink curve inside each ear, like
the picture. Use a small brush
and thin coats. Rinse and blot
your brush. Let the pink dry.
5. Bring her face to life
Paint the nostrils brown. Use
black for the pupils, lashes,
eyebrows, smile, and fur lines.
Keep the eye crescents and
shine dots white. Let black dry.
Add white to tidy the eye shines.
Take your time with a small
brush.
6. Dry and clean up
Check for small white gaps and
cover them with the matching
paint. Lay your finished canvas
flat to dry. Close the paint pots.
Rinse your brushes, blot them
gently, and wipe your work area.
Your Highland cow is finished!
Thin coats - Dry between colors - Have fun!
