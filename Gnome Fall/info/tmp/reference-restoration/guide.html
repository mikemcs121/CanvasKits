Gnome Fall

Acrylic Painting Guide

Materials: Preprinted 8 × 10 canvas • Acrylic paints • Large and small brushes
Water cup • Paper towels • Palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, red, yellow, teal, green, brown, orange

1. Begin with the outline

Protect your table. Follow the printed lines; use thin coats. Rinse and blot brushes between colors.

2. Brush in the teal sky

Mix teal with a little white. Paint around the printed shapes with short strokes.

3. Ground & branches

Paint branches and stems brown. Mix green with a little brown for the ground; leave leaves white.

4. Fill the curling hat

Mix red with a little brown for rust. Paint thin curved strokes that follow the folds.

5. Sleeves & leather

Mix green with a little brown for sleeves. Paint boots brown; add a speck of black along lower edges.

6. Lay in autumn color

Mix orange with a touch of brown for pumpkins and the maple leaf. Alternate orange and yellow on other leaves.

7. Skin & soft beard

For peach, mix white, a little yellow and a tiny touch of red. Paint nose and hands. Mix white with a touch of yellow for the beard.

8. Light the lanterns

Paint frames, chains and rings black + a little brown. Use yellow in the glass; add white to yellow for flames and a soft glow.

9. Shape the hat

Mix red, brown and a speck of black for fold shadows. Mix red with a little yellow for highlights. Blend along curves.

10. Round the pumpkins

Mix orange with brown for groove shadows. Mix orange with a little yellow for highlights; follow the ribs.

11. Pull fine beard strands

Mix white, a touch of teal and a speck of brown for shadows. Pull strokes toward the tips; add white and a little yellow highlights.

12. Dry & clean up

Let the canvas dry flat. Wash and reshape brushes, wipe the palette and close paint pots.

Thin coats • Let layers dry • Enjoy painting
