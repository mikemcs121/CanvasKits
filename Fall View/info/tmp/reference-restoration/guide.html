Fall View

Acrylic Painting Guide

Materials: Preprinted 8 × 10 canvas • Acrylic paints • Large and small brushes
Water cup • Paper towels • Palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, red, yellow, blue, green, brown, orange

1. Set up

Cover the table. Start with the printed outline. Use thin coats; rinse and blot brushes between colors.

2. Blend the sky

Mix white with blue for the upper sky. Below, blend yellow into orange, then red plus white. Work horizontally around the outlines.

3. Light the sunset

Mix white with yellow for the sun and bright cloud streaks. Mix white with red for softer streaks. Let the sky dry.

4. Layer distant ridges

Mix blue with white for the far ridges. Use less white in each nearer ridge. Follow the printed curves; let adjoining bands dry.

5. Deepen the valley

Mix blue with green and a little black. Fill the nearest forest slopes. Tap upward with the small brush for pointed treetops.

6. Block in the rocks

Mix black, brown and a little white. Brush this gray-brown base across the rock slabs. Leave the printed fallen leaves white.

7. Paint the tree

Mix brown with a little black. Fill the trunk and branches with lengthwise strokes. Keep the foliage white. Let the base dry.

8. Build autumn color

Mix red with a little brown. Dab into the canopy and shrubs. Add orange over most areas, leaving darker pockets visible.

9. Lift the leaves

Mix orange with yellow. Tap small clusters along the lit foliage edges, leaving gaps. Use red, orange and yellow on fallen leaves.

10. Catch the bark light

Mix brown with yellow and a little white. Drag a nearly dry small brush along the trunk's left edge and branch tops.

11. Define the ledges

Mix white with a little black and brown. Skim the upper rock edges with a nearly dry brush. Keep the dark cracks visible.

12. Dry and clean up

Lay the canvas flat until fully dry. Wash and reshape brushes, wipe the palette and close the paint pots.

Thin coats • Let layers dry • Enjoy the view
