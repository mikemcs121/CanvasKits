# Fall View - editable step plan

Source: preserved composite. Finished reference and outline are generated reconstructions. Current target: fall-view-finished-reference.png.

Materials: preprinted canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes, water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate.
Paint pots: white, black, red, yellow, blue, green, brown, orange.

## 1. Set up
Cover the table. Start with the printed outline. Use thin coats; rinse and blot brushes between colors.
Illustration / cumulative state: Exact supplied outline, all regions white.

## 2. Blend the sky
Mix white with blue for the upper sky. Below, blend yellow into orange, then red plus white. Work horizontally around the outlines.
Illustration / cumulative state: Only sky painted, sun, mountains, all trees and rocks white.

## 3. Light the sunset
Mix white with yellow for the sun and bright cloud streaks. Mix white with red for softer streaks. Let the sky dry.
Illustration / cumulative state: Sky and sun painted; all other regions white.

## 4. Layer distant ridges
Mix blue with white for the far ridges. Use less white in each nearer ridge. Follow the printed curves; let adjoining bands dry.
Illustration / cumulative state: All middle distance ridges blue in increasingly darker layers. Forest, rocks, tree, shrubs white.

## 5. Deepen the valley
Mix blue with green and a little black. Fill the nearest forest slopes. Tap upward with the small brush for pointed treetops.
Illustration / cumulative state: Add dark near forest bands and pointed treetops; large tree foliage shrubs rocks white.

## 6. Block in the rocks
Mix black, brown and a little white. Brush this gray-brown base across the rock slabs. Leave the printed fallen leaves white.
Illustration / cumulative state: Add foreground gray brown rock, leaves white. Tree and foliage white.

## 7. Paint the tree
Mix brown with a little black. Fill the trunk and branches with lengthwise strokes. Keep the foliage white. Let the base dry.
Illustration / cumulative state: Add dark brown trunk and branches precisely in outline geometry; foliage and fallen leaves white.

## 8. Build autumn color
Mix red with a little brown. Dab into the canopy and shrubs. Add orange over most areas, leaving darker pockets visible.
Illustration / cumulative state: Add rust red orange canopy and both shrub masses; fallen leaves still white.

## 9. Lift the leaves
Mix orange with yellow. Tap small clusters along the lit foliage edges, leaving gaps. Use red, orange and yellow on fallen leaves.
Illustration / cumulative state: Close-up upper left canopy showing brush dabbing golden highlights over red orange masses.

## 10. Catch the bark light
Mix brown with yellow and a little white. Drag a nearly dry small brush along the trunk's left edge and branch tops.
Illustration / cumulative state: Close-up right trunk and branching junction, brush lengthwise highlight, final foliage visible.

## 11. Define the ledges
Mix white with a little black and brown. Skim the upper rock edges with a nearly dry brush. Keep the dark cracks visible.
Illustration / cumulative state: Close-up rock ledges and painted fallen leaves; wide flat brush skimming highlights.

## 12. Dry and clean up
Lay the canvas flat until fully dry. Wash and reshape brushes, wipe the palette and close the paint pots.
Illustration / cumulative state: Final exact finished painting lying flat on a table in overhead view, capped paint pots and clean brushes alongside.
