Cat in Pumpkin
Acrylic Painting Guide
 Materials: Preprinted 8 x 10 canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes
 Water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, red, yellow, blue, brown, orange
 Use thin coats. Rinse your brush and pat it dry when you change colors.
1. Get ready
Cover your table. Follow the lines on your canvas.
Leave the background white.
Use your small brush to paint the eye centers and
the cat's mouth black. Leave a small white dot in
each eye. Keep the tongue white. Let it dry.
2. Paint the pumpkin
Use your large brush to paint the pumpkin and its top
edge orange. Use a thin coat.
Leave the face holes, the opening behind the cat,
and the cat white. Let the paint dry.
3. Paint the cat
Mix black with white and a tiny bit of blue to make
gray. Paint the cat's head, paws and tail.
Leave the inside of the ears, nose, tongue and white
parts of the eyes white. Let the paint dry.
4. Add the little details
Pink: Mix white, a little red and a tiny bit of brown.
Paint inside the ears, the nose and tongue.
Gold: Mix yellow with a little brown. Paint the curved
eye edges.
Dark brown: Mix black with a little brown. Fill the
pumpkin's face holes and opening behind the cat.
Keep teeth orange.
Let it dry. With your small brush, follow the lines and
whiskers in black. Add white eye dots.
 Lay your canvas flat to dry. Wash your brushes and close the paint pots.
