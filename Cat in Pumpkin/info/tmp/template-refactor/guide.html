Cat in Pumpkin

Acrylic Painting Guide

Materials: Preprinted 8 × 10 canvas • Acrylic paints • Large and small brushes
Water cup • Paper towels • Palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, red, yellow, blue, brown, orange

1. Start with the outline

Protect your table. Follow the preprinted lines. Keep the background white. Use thin coats; rinse and blot between colors.

2. Orange pumpkin base

Paint the pumpkin shell and rim orange in thin coats. Leave the carved face, opening and kitten white.

3. Charcoal kitten

Mix black with white and a touch of blue for charcoal gray. Paint the head, paws and curled tail; leave inner ears and nose clear.

4. Rose ears & nose

Mix white with a little red and a tiny touch of brown for dusty rose. Paint the inner ears, nose and tongue.

5. Golden eyes

Mix yellow with a little brown for gold. Fill the small curved eye rims; keep the black pupils and white glints distinct.

6. Deep carved shapes

Mix black with a little brown. Fill the pumpkin eyes, nose and mouth, plus the opening behind the cat. Keep the teeth orange.

7. Round the pumpkin

Mix orange with brown for the grooves. Mix orange with yellow for rib highlights. Curve each stroke down the pumpkin.

8. Soft directional fur

Mix white, black and a touch of blue for lighter gray. Pull short strokes over the dry charcoal, following cheeks and tail.

9. Light along the rim

Mix orange with yellow and a little white. Trace a narrow highlight on the front rim and the upper edges of pumpkin ribs.

10. Small paw highlights

Mix white with a little black and brown for warm gray. Add short strokes on the paw tops; keep the toe divisions darker.

11. Whiskers & eye shine

Let the face dry. Use black and a fine brush to tidy whiskers and the smile. Add a small white dot to each eye.

12. Dry & clean up

Lay the canvas flat until fully dry. Wash brushes, wipe the palette and close the paint pots.

Thin coats • Let layers dry • Enjoy painting
