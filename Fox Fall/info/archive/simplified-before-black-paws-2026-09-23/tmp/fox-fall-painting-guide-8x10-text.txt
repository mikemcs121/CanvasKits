Autumn Fox
Simplified Acrylic Painting Guide
 Materials: Preprinted 8 x 10 canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes
 Water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate
Paint colors provided: orange, ivory, forest green, golden yellow, dark brown, black, white
 Use thin coats. Rinse your brush and pat it dry when you change colors. Let each color dry before painting beside it.
1. Get ready
Cover your table. Start with the
preprinted fox canvas. Keep the
background white.
Use thin coats. Rinse your brush and
pat it dry when you change colors.
2. Paint the orange fur
Use orange for the head, outer ears,
body and lower tail.
Leave the face patches, chest, tail tip,
scarf, paws and leaves white. Let it
dry.
3. Add the ivory fur
Use ivory for the face patches, inner
ears, chest and tail tip.
Keep the scarf, paws and leaves
white. Let the paint dry.
4. Paint the scarf
Paint every other scarf stripe forest
green.
Paint the open stripes golden yellow.
Keep the black dividing lines easy to
see.
5. Paint the leaves
Paint the maple leaf orange. Paint the
oak leaf golden yellow.
Use your small brush near the edges.
Let the leaves dry.
6. Finish the little details
Use dark brown for the paws and ear
tips.
Use black for the eyes, nose, smile
and leaf stems. Keep or add a tiny
white dot in each eye.
 Lay your canvas flat to dry. Wash your brushes and close the paint pots.
