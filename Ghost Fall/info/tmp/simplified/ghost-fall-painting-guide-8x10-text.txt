Autumn Ghost
Simplified Acrylic Painting Guide
 Materials: Preprinted 8 x 10 canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes
 Water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate
Paint colors provided: ivory, plum purple, orange, olive green, coral pink, black
 Use thin coats. Rinse your brush and pat it dry when you change colors. Let each color dry before painting beside it.
1. Get ready
Cover your table. Start with the
preprinted ghost canvas. Keep the
background white.
Use thin coats. Rinse your brush
and pat it dry when you change
colors.
2. Paint the ghost
Use ivory for the ghost and both
hands. Paint around the eyes,
mouth and pumpkin.
Leave the bow, pumpkin, stem,
cheeks and tongue white. Let the
paint dry.
3. Paint the bow
Use plum purple for the bow loops,
knot and ribbon tails.
Use a small brush near the black
lines. Let the bow dry.
4. Add the pumpkin
Paint the pumpkin orange. Follow each curved section
from top to bottom.
Paint the stem olive green. Keep the ghost's hands
ivory.
5. Finish the face
Paint the cheeks and tongue coral pink.
Use black for the eyes, eyebrows and mouth. Keep the
tiny eye dots white. Lay the canvas flat to dry.
 Lay your canvas flat to dry. Wash your brushes and close the paint pots.
