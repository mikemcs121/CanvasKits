Christmas Gnome

Acrylic Painting Guide

Materials: Preprinted 8 × 10 canvas • Acrylic paints • Large and small brushes
Water cup • Paper towels • Palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, red, yellow, blue, green, brown

1. Set up your canvas

Cover your table. Follow the printed outlines. Rinse and blot your brush between colors.

2. Paint the background

Mix white with a touch of blue for the sky. Paint snow white. Work around printed shapes.

3. Paint the trees

Paint trees green; keep lights white. Mix green + blue + white for the small distant tree. Use downward strokes; add green + yellow highlights.

4. Paint hat and coat

Use red in thin layers. Leave the nose, beard, pom-pom and light circles white.

5. Add nose and shoes

For peach, mix white, a little yellow and a tiny touch of red. Paint the nose peach and the shoes brown.

6. Paint the fluffy whites

Paint beard and pom-pom white. For shadows, mix white, a touch of blue and a speck of black.

7. Shape the hat folds

Mix red with a little brown for shadows. Blend along the hat folds. Add red with a touch of yellow for highlights. Let dry.

8. Light up the hat

Paint bulbs yellow over a dry white base. Add white shine dots. Use green and a speck of black for the cord.

9. Paint the tree lights

Use blue and yellow. Let dry, then add tiny white dots to make the ornaments shine.

10. Brighten the snow

Mix white with a touch of blue for snow shadows. Add white highlights to snow and beard.

11. Refine the pom-pom

Dab white over the pom-pom's blue shadows. Let dry before fixing edges. Add a few white snow dots to the hat if you like.

12. Let it dry

Lay your canvas flat until dry. Wash brushes, close paint pots and clean your palette.

Thin coats • Let layers dry • Enjoy painting
