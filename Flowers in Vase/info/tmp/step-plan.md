# Flowers in Vase - editable stage plan

Audience: adult beginner acrylic paint night. Source photograph retained unchanged. The smoother companion outline is a generated reinterpretation, not a trace of the impasto edges. Finished reference was colored from that locked outline.

Palette: white, black, turquoise, blue, green, rose red, yellow, orange, brown.
Materials: preprinted canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes, water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate. Contents and canvas dimensions are not asserted.

Geometry master: flowers-in-vase-outline.png, 1254 x 1254 pixels. Region IDs and bounding boxes in regions.csv; exact masks in regions.bin. Group assignments and editable captions in build.cs.

1. Printed outline only. All regions white.
2. Turquoise background revealed. Bouquet and vase remain white.
3. Greenery base fills. Flowers, berries and vase remain white.
4. Alternating navy and white vase base fills. All flowers and berries remain white.
5. Blush base fills for upper-left/lower-right blooms. Their centers remain white.
6. Apricot upper-right flower base fill. Its center remains white.
7. Rose-red central flower base fill. Its center remains white.
8. Golden small flowers and centers base fills. Berries remain white.
9. Reveal reference shadows/highlights for all large flower petals. Lower-right bloom crop with brush teaches curved layering. Greenery and vase still base coats; berries white.
10. Reveal greenery and vase reference texture/highlights. Crop includes left leaves and vase; brush shows curved streaks. Berries white.
11. Reveal gold texture and berry fills/highlights. Right-hand berry crop with brush teaches shine dots.
12. Exact finished reference on a flat canvas, closed paint pots and clean brushes nearby.

Background texture is introduced in step 2. Other base coats remain untextured until their finishing step. Full-canvas stages reuse the exact outline line pixels. Close-ups are crops of staged masters; brush cues are separate document illustration elements.
