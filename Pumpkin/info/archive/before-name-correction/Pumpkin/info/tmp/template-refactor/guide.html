Autumn Pumpkin

Acrylic Painting Guide

Materials: Preprinted 8 × 10 canvas • Acrylic paints • Large and small brushes
Water cup • Paper towels • Palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, yellow, green, brown, orange

1. Start with the outline

Protect your table. Follow the preprinted lines. Keep the background white. Use thin coats; rinse and blot between colors.

2. An even orange base

Paint the pumpkin orange in two thin coats, drying between coats. Leave the stem and carved face white; keep both teeth orange.

3. Olive-green stem

Mix green with brown for olive. Paint the stem, following its bend. Keep the outer printed contour clear.

4. The carved smile

Mix black with a little brown. Fill the eyes, nose and mouth carefully around the orange teeth. Let these dark shapes dry.

5. Shape the grooves

Mix orange with brown for russet shadows. Pull narrow curved strokes beside the printed grooves and around the pumpkin base.

6. Golden rib highlights

Mix orange with yellow and a little white. Pull lighter strokes down the raised ribs, leaving the shaded grooves visible.

7. Stem & finishing edges

Mix yellow with brown and a little white for stem highlights. Pull strokes along the bend; tidy carved edges with black and brown.

8. Dry & clean up

Lay the canvas flat until fully dry. Wash brushes, wipe the palette and close the paint pots.

Thin coats • Let layers dry • Enjoy painting
