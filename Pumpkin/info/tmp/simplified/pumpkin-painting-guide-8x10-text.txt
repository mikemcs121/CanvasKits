Autumn Pumpkin
Simplified Acrylic Painting Guide
 Materials: Preprinted 8 x 10 canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes
 Water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate
Paint colors provided: orange, olive green, black
 Use thin coats. Rinse your brush and pat it dry when you change colors. Let each color dry before painting beside it.
1. Get ready
Cover your table. Start with the preprinted pumpkin
canvas. Keep the background white.
Use thin coats. Rinse your brush and pat it dry when
you change colors.
2. Paint the pumpkin
Use orange for every pumpkin section. Paint around
the eyes, nose and mouth.
Paint both teeth orange. Leave the stem and face
openings white. Let the paint dry.
3. Paint the stem
Use olive green for the stem. Follow its bend with your
brush.
Use a small brush near the black lines. Let the stem
dry.
4. Finish the face
Use black for both eyes, the nose and the mouth. Paint
carefully around the orange teeth.
Check the edges, then lay the canvas flat to dry.
 Lay your canvas flat to dry. Wash your brushes and close the paint pots.
