# Starry Night Sunflower

Materials: preprinted canvas, acrylic paints, large and small brushes, water cup, paper towels, palette or paper plate.
Paint pots: white, black, blue, yellow, orange, green, brown (seven).
Source: supplied composite; clean companions are generated reconstructions.

## 1. Start with the outline
Protect your table. Follow the preprinted lines. Use thin coats; rinse and blot brushes between colors.
Illustration/progression: Show exact unpainted supplied outline.

## 2. Lay in the night sky
Mix blue with a little black. Paint only the sky around the flowers and greenery. Keep all plant shapes white.
Illustration/progression: Same outline with dark blue sky only. All petals, centers, stems and leaves white.

## 3. Turn the blue swirls
Mix blue with white. Follow the sky curves with short strokes, leaving darker gaps. Let dry.
Illustration/progression: Same stage adding blue-white sky swirls; all plant shapes still white.

## 4. Light the stars
Mix white with a little yellow. Dab star centers and curved light trails. Keep some blue showing.
Illustration/progression: Sky complete including pale yellow stars; all plant shapes white.

## 5. Block in both flowers
Paint every petal yellow, from its base toward its tip. Keep seed heads and greenery white. Let dry.
Illustration/progression: Sky complete. Both flowers petals yellow only. Centers and all greenery white.

## 6. Warm the petal folds
Brush orange along petal bases and one side of each fold. Blend into yellow with outward strokes.
Illustration/progression: Add orange shadows to both flowers petals. Centers and greenery still white.

## 7. Paint stems and leaves
Mix green with a little blue. Fill stems, leaves and curled flower backs, following their length.
Illustration/progression: Add dark green foliage to same composition. Both seed heads still white.

## 8. Ground the seed heads
Mix brown with a little black. Fill both seed heads; use small dabs along the rims. Let dry.
Illustration/progression: Fill both seed heads dark brown; complete base painting.

## 9. Shape the greenery
Mix green with yellow for light edges. Mix green with blue and a speck of black for shadows. Pull curved strokes.
Illustration/progression: Useful tight close-up of curved left stem and leaves with brush laying light edge.

## 10. Lift the petals
Mix yellow with a little white. Pull slender highlights from base to tip on dry petals; leave orange folds visible.
Illustration/progression: Useful tight close-up of large flower petals with brush following pointed petal.

## 11. Stipple the seeds
Dab brown, orange and yellow onto dry seed heads. Mix white with yellow for a few bright dots. Keep dark gaps.
Illustration/progression: Useful tight close-up of seed head with stippling brush. White-yellow means white mixed with yellow explicitly change caption below.

## 12. Dry and clean up
Let the canvas dry flat. Wash and reshape brushes, wipe the palette and close all paint pots.
Illustration/progression: Completed exact target square canvas lying FLAT viewed overhead with closed pots and clean brushes. No easel.
