Acrylic Painting Guide

Materials: Preprinted 8 × 10 canvas • Acrylic paints • Large and small brushes
Water cup • Paper towels • Palette or paper plate
Paints: white, black, blue, yellow, orange, green, brown

1. Prepare your space

Protect your table. Follow the preprinted lines. Use thin coats; rinse and blot brushes between colors.

2. Lay in the sky

Mix blue with a little black. Paint only the sky around the flowers and greenery. Keep all plant shapes white.

3. Turn the blue swirls

Mix blue with white. Follow the sky curves with short strokes, leaving darker gaps. Let dry.

4. Light the stars

Mix white with a little yellow. Dab star centers and curved light trails. Keep some blue showing.

5. Paint both flowers

Paint every petal yellow, from its base toward its tip. Keep seed heads and greenery white. Let dry.

6. Warm the folds

Brush orange along petal bases and one side of each fold. Blend into yellow with outward strokes.

7. Paint stems and leaves

Mix green with a little blue. Fill stems, leaves and curled flower backs, following their length.

8. Fill the seed heads

Mix brown with a little black. Fill both seed heads; use small dabs along the rims. Let dry.

9. Shape the greenery

Mix green with yellow for light edges. Mix green with blue and a speck of black for shadows. Pull curved strokes.

10. Lift the petals

Mix yellow with a little white. Pull slender highlights from base to tip on dry petals; leave orange folds visible.

11. Stipple the seeds

Dab brown, orange and yellow onto dry seed heads. Mix white with yellow for a few bright dots. Keep dark gaps.

12. Dry and clean up

Lay the canvas flat until fully dry. Wash and reshape brushes, wipe the palette and close the paint pots.

Thin coats • Let layers dry • Enjoy painting
