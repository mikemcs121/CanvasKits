## Standing composition requirement

For new builds and requested revisions, the subject arrangement itself must read as 8 x 10 portrait when viewed as an outline on transparency. Extra sky, ground or margins around a square arrangement do not satisfy this requirement. The file dimensions above describe existing exports; they are not evidence of a corrected portrait composition. Follow the composition gate in `.agents/skills/build-canvas/SKILL.md` and `AGENTS.md` before promoting future revisions.

## Current transfer-line revision

All four listed kits now have fine 1.4-point gray vector transfer outlines with transparent backgrounds and physical size 8 x 10 inches. Fox Fall and Cat in a Pumpkin use #808080 for children; Ghost Fall and Pumpkin use #A6A6A6. Current SVG links and all ten kit statuses are in [canvas-transfer-outlines.md](canvas-transfer-outlines.md). Existing centered placements and painting guides are unchanged in this linework-only revision; full portrait recomposition is a separate pending choice. Prior bold exports are archived under each kit's tmp/transfer-standard/before-transfer folder.
